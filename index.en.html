A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

A few helpful answers

Visit support
Do I need an account or internet connection?+

No app account is needed. Games and photo packs work offline. Ads, Google Play purchases and restoration, website visits, and external sharing may need internet. Personal photos and game records are not uploaded to a developer server.

Where are my photos stored?+

Only photos you choose are copied into private app storage; originals stay unchanged. Add up to 50 personal photos across your packs. Keep your original photos before uninstalling or clearing app data.

How does together mode work?+

Choose names for 2–4 players and a shared difficulty, then pass one device around. Each person gets different questions with the same difficulty, question count, and scoring rules. Compare results at the end.

Can I remove ads?+

Settings → Play without ads offers a one-time Google Play purchase, not a subscription. Check the purchase screen for price and availability. Restore with the same Google account; restoration does not recover photos or game progress.
