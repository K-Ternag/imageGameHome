1. No app account to close

RememPic has no app account or online profile to close. Local data, shared files, Google Play purchases and support records are managed separately below.

2. Delete photos or packs

  1. Open a personal pack in Photo packs.
  2. Select a photo and choose Delete photo from app, or choose Delete pack.
  3. Only app copies are deleted. Gallery originals remain.

3. Reset game records

Choose Home → Settings → Reset game records → Delete records. This deletes records and record badges. Unlocked levels, stars and packs remain. To remove all progress, follow the next steps.

4. Clear all app data

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → 리멤픽 (RememPic) → Storage.
  2. Confirm Clear data / Clear storage or uninstall. Menu names vary by device.
  3. This removes photo copies, packs, levels, stars, records, together-mode names, settings, local purchase status and event logs.
Clearing the cache alone does not remove all data.

Photo packs and game progress have no backup or restore feature. Keep gallery originals separately.

5. Originals, shared files and ad information

Gallery originals and exported or shared result cards can remain after app data is cleared. Manage them in the gallery, file manager or sharing service. Copies already received by others are not deleted automatically.

Change ad privacy choices in app settings where offered. External providers’ records are separate from app data; use the providers’ policies and request channels.

6. Purchases, restoration and refunds

Uninstalling does not delete Google Play transactions or refund a purchase. With the purchasing Google account, choose Settings → Play without ads → Restore purchase. This does not restore photo packs or game progress.

Manage purchases through Google Play, consult its refund guidance or contact greatryuhg@gmail.com.

7. Delete support records

Email greatryuhg@gmail.com with RememPic support data deletion in the subject. Provide the sending address and approximate date of your earlier message. Do not send passwords, identity documents or personal photos.

Support messages are kept for 30 days after resolution. At the end of this period or when fulfilling a verified deletion request, the operator permanently deletes emails and attachments from the mailbox and trash, including separately saved attachment copies. Provider system records and backups follow the email provider’s policy.

We review requests and respond without undue delay. If verification or more time is needed, we explain why and provide an expected timeline.

The support page prepares a draft. You must send it from your email app; the website cannot remotely clear your device.
