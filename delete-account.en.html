1. No account to close

Photo Games currently has no registration, sign-in, or server accounts. You can delete photos and game data directly on your device without closing an account. The developer holds no app account or uploaded photos.

2. Delete a photo or pack

  1. Open Photo packs → Manage my photo packs and choose a pack.
  2. Tap a photo and choose Delete photo from app, or select Delete photo pack.
  3. Confirm the deletion. Copies inside the app are removed; original photos in your gallery are kept.

3. Reset game records

From Home, choose Settings → Reset game records → Delete records. This removes records and record badges. Unlocked levels, stars, and photo packs remain. Use the full deletion steps below to remove all progress.

4. Delete all app data

  1. In Android, open Settings → Apps → 사진게임 → Storage.
  2. Choose Clear data or Clear storage and confirm, or uninstall the app. Menu names vary by device.
  3. This removes photo copies, pack names, levels, stars, records, solo/together/challenge state, participant names, and settings held in the app.
Clearing the cache alone does not delete all app data.

There is no cloud backup or restore feature, so progress cannot be restored after full deletion.

5. Data managed elsewhere

Original gallery photos, exported result cards, and challenge codes or messages sent to friends are not removed by clearing app data. Manage them in the gallery, file manager, or sharing service. Recipients may retain files and messages already sent to them.

6. Delete support correspondence

Request deletion of information sent to support at greatryuhg@gmail.com. Use “Photo Games support data deletion” as the subject and provide the sending email address and approximate date of your earlier message. Do not send passwords, personal photos, or identity documents.

Support retention: The retention period and deletion procedure for support messages are awaiting confirmation.
Response information: Response times are awaiting confirmation.

This website cannot delete data from your device. The support page can prepare an email draft; you must send it yourself for it to reach the operator.
