1. Service and availability

RememPic offers eight photo games, photo packs, records and pass-and-play on one device. These terms cover the app and website. The operator is K-Terang; contact greatryuhg@gmail.com. Android 9 or later is required. The current app UI is Korean.

2. Photos and content rights

Choose only photos you are entitled to use and respect others’ privacy, image and copyright rights. Personal photos remain with their owners; app use does not transfer ownership to the developer. App design, code and built-in content remain subject to their respective owners’ rights. Photo credits are available in app settings.

3. Games, records and sharing

Records are stored locally; scores and completion criteria depend on the game, level and rules. Together mode takes turns on one device. After sharing, files are managed by you, recipients and external services. This is an entertainment app and does not promise medical diagnosis or cognitive improvement.

4. Advertising and one-time purchases

Interstitial ads may appear between games. A one-time Google Play purchase removes ads; it is not an auto-renewing subscription. Confirm price, currency, taxes and availability on the actual purchase screen. Pending payments receive benefits once completed and verified. Minors should review purchases with a guardian.

5. Restoration and refunds

Restore with the same Google account. Restoration checks ad-removal access, not photos or progress. Retry connection or verification errors or contact greatryuhg@gmail.com.

Refunds follow Google Play’s refund policy and applicable law. Request through purchase history or contact the operator. Uninstalling does not automatically refund purchases; refunded or revoked purchases may lose benefits on the next verification. Statutory consumer rights are not limited.

6. Storage and ending use

Uninstalling, clearing data or changing devices may remove photo packs and progress. No cloud backup is provided for that data. Keep original photos separately. End use by uninstalling; see the data deletion guide for each category.

7. Changes and responsibility

Screens and functionality can vary by device, operating system and version. Important service changes will be announced in the app or on this website. These terms do not limit statutory consumer rights or the operator’s legal obligations.
