Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account or internet connection?+

No app account is needed. Games and photo packs work offline. Ads, Google Play purchases and restoration, website visits, and external sharing may need internet. Personal photos and game records are not uploaded to a developer server.

Where are my photos stored?+

Only photos you choose are copied into private app storage; originals stay unchanged. Add up to 50 personal photos across your packs. Keep your original photos before uninstalling or clearing app data.

How does together mode work?+

Choose names for 2–4 players and a shared difficulty, then pass one device around. Each person gets different questions with the same difficulty, question count, and scoring rules. Compare results at the end.

Can I remove ads?+

Settings → Play without ads offers a one-time Google Play purchase, not a subscription. Check the purchase screen for price and availability. Restore with the same Google account; restoration does not recover photos or game progress.

Will progress transfer to a new device?+

There is no cloud sync or backup for photo packs and game records. Uninstalling or clearing app data removes local progress. An ad-removal purchase can be restored separately with the same Google account.

How do I earn stars and unlock levels?+

Each game has 100 levels. Check the level instructions for its clear and star requirements. Using a hint can count as practice without new stars or the next level. Regular levels are untimed; every tenth level is a timed challenge.

Can I share a result or the app?+

Use the share icon on a result or Share the app in Settings. Result cards exclude personal photos. Choose the destination and review the message in the Android share sheet.

My purchase has not removed ads.+

Check your purchasing Google account and connection, then choose Settings → Play without ads → Restore purchase. Pending payments apply after completion. If it persists, email your app version and the error. Do not send card numbers or passwords.

How can I request a refund?+

Request a refund through Google Play purchase history or contact support. Eligibility depends on the purchase, timing, and local rules. Uninstalling does not automatically refund a purchase.

Which devices and languages are supported?+

Android 9 and later are supported. Current app screens are in Korean; this website is available in Korean and English. A download link will appear when the public Google Play listing is confirmed. Questions and scores in the images are examples.
