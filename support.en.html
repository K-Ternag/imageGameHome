Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account or internet connection?+

The current app works on your device without an account. Games and built-in photo packs work offline. Installation, this website, and sharing through other apps may require a connection.

Where are my photos stored?+

Only photos you select are copied into the app’s private storage. Originals stay unchanged and photos are not uploaded to a developer server. You can add up to 50 personal photos across all your packs.

How does together mode work?+

Choose names for 2–4 players and a shared difficulty, then pass one device around. Each player gets different questions with the same difficulty, question count, and scoring rules. This is pass-and-play, not live online multiplayer.

How do friend challenges work?+

Share a challenge and its code from a completed solo game using built-in photos. Your friend pastes the code into Friend challenge on the game selection screen to play the same questions and compare scores. Results are not sent back automatically. Games using personal photos are not supported.

Will progress transfer to a new device?+

There is currently no cloud sync or in-app backup. Photo packs, levels, stars, and games in progress are stored on the device and are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Is there a time limit?+

Regular levels have no completion time limit; every tenth level is a timed challenge. Memory games still show photos for a set time. Together mode has no completion time limit.

Which devices are supported? Is it available?+

The app supports Android 9 and later. A download button will link to Google Play once the public listing is confirmed. Website images are based on development builds; English versions are translated promotional adaptations and may differ from the release.
