1. Scope

This policy covers the 사진게임 / Photo Games Android app and its website. The reviewed app version is 0.8.0. The app currently has no registration, sign-in, or developer backend. On-device processing is described separately from website visits and email support.

2. Data and purposes

DataPurpose and storage

3. Photo access and permissions

The app reads only photos you select with the Android system photo picker. It does not request full photo-library, location, contacts, microphone, or camera access. Selected photos are copied into private app storage as JPEG files with a maximum long edge of 1,200px. Orientation is applied and GPS/EXIF metadata is removed from the copies. Original photos are unchanged. Color and composition analysis also happens on your device.

The current app does not declare the internet permission and does not upload personal photos or game records to a developer server. Photos may include identifiable people; choose images you are entitled to use.

4. Sharing, services, and advertising

The current app contains no advertising, billing, analytics, or remote crash-reporting SDKs. It does not sell app data or provide it for advertising. It has no external authentication or cloud-sync service.

If you share a result card or challenge code, the selected app and recipient process what you share. Result images exclude personal photos. Challenge codes contain information for reproducing questions with built-in photos and comparing scores, without a server lookup. The selected sharing service applies its own policy.

5. Retention and deletion

Local photos, settings, progress, and records remain on the device until deleted or app data is cleared. Deleting a personal photo or pack removes its copies from app storage. Resetting game records removes records and record badges but keeps unlocked levels, stars, and photo packs. Clear app data in Android settings or uninstall the app to remove all local app data. There is no server backup or in-app cloud restore.

Together-mode state is replaced by later sessions or removed when leaving that mode through its exit flow. Clear app data to remove all saved states. Original gallery photos and externally saved or shared files must be managed separately.

Support-message retention and deletion: The retention period and deletion procedure for support messages are awaiting confirmation.

See the data deletion guide for instructions.

6. Your rights and guardians

You can view and remove personal photos in the app and clear all app data through Android settings. Contact us below to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of information you sent to support. Minimal identity verification may be necessary. The developer cannot remotely access or delete photos and game records held only on your device.

Guardians can help children choose photos, manage sharing, and contact the same support address. The app does not ask for accounts, ages, or dates of birth. Do not attach a child’s personal photos or sensitive information to support requests.

7. Safeguards

Photo copies and game data are held in Android private app storage. Android automatic app backup is disabled. Sharing grants the selected destination access to the shared file. This website has no photo upload or account registration. You can also manage your device screen lock and externally shared files.

8. Website and email support

This website uses no site-operated analytics, advertising scripts, tracking cookies, or external web fonts. GitHub may process IP addresses and technical request information when hosting it through GitHub Pages. Locations, retention, and rights are described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The support composer opens a draft in your email app. It does not submit or store your text on a website server. Your email providers and the operator process it only after you send it. Consult the selected external service’s processing locations and policy.
