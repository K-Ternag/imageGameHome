1. Scope and operator

This policy covers the 리멤픽 / RememPic Android app and this website. The operator is K-Terang; contact greatryuhg@gmail.com for privacy requests. The reviewed version is 0.9.0. There is no app account or developer backend. Local photo and game data are distinguished from advertising, purchases, hosting, and email processing.

3. Photos and permissions

The Android photo picker reads only your selections. The app does not request full gallery, location, contacts, microphone or camera access. Photos are copied as JPEGs with a maximum long edge of 1,200px after orientation is applied; GPS/EXIF metadata is not retained in the copies. Originals are unchanged. Photo color and composition analysis happens on the device.

Internet and network-state permissions support ads and purchase verification. Personal photos, game records and participant names are not uploaded to a developer server or attached to ad requests.

4. Ads and privacy choices

Google AdMob interstitial ads may appear between games. Google Mobile Ads SDK may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), launches, taps, ad interactions, performance and diagnostic data, and device identifiers depending on settings, for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Removing advertising-ID permissions does not eliminate all advertising data processing. See Google’s advertising SDK disclosure.

The app checks whether consent status permits ad requests before preparing ads. Where available, use Ad privacy settings in Settings to change choices. Verified ad removal stops between-game ads; purchase verification can continue.

5. Children and guardians

The app does not request ages, dates of birth or app accounts. Builds configured for children use Google’s CHILD treatment to restrict personalized advertising, remarketing and advertising-ID transmission, with ad content limited to G. Release preparation is ongoing; the child-directed configuration of the release build remains to be verified before publication.

Guardians should help children manage photo selection, external sharing and Google Play purchases. Guardians may request access to or deletion of support information at the email below. Do not attach children’s personal photos, identity documents or sensitive information.

6. Google Play purchases

Ad removal is a one-time Google Play product. The app uses product IDs, purchase tokens, purchase states and signed purchase information to verify, acknowledge and restore purchases, storing the last verified access state locally. The developer does not directly collect card numbers, bank details or Google passwords. Local event logs exclude purchase tokens, receipts, emails and photo paths.

Google Play payment and transaction records follow Google’s policies and are not removed by uninstalling. See the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Sharing and external services

Sharing a result card or app introduction sends the chosen image or message to the selected app and recipient. Result cards exclude personal photos. The app has no photo or progress cloud sync, separate developer analytics, or remote crash-reporting service. Advertising measurement and diagnostics are described separately in section 4.

8. Retention and deletion

Local photos, preferences and progress remain until deleted. Removing a photo or pack deletes its app copies. Resetting game records removes records and record badges but retains unlocked levels, stars and packs. Clear Android app data or uninstall to remove all local data. Ad and billing event history keeps up to 80 recent events, replacing older entries as new ones arrive.

Support records: Support messages are kept for 30 days after resolution. At the end of this period or when fulfilling a verified deletion request, the operator permanently deletes emails and attachments from the mailbox and trash, including separately saved attachment copies. Provider system records and backups follow the email provider’s policy.

Google and GitHub apply their own purpose-specific retention and deletion policies to transactions, security records, access logs and backups. No single retention period applies to all external records. See the data deletion guide.

9. Rights and requests

Users and guardians can manage photos and packs locally and request access, correction, deletion or restriction of information sent to the operator at greatryuhg@gmail.com. We request only the information needed to verify the request and explain any limits. We review requests and respond without undue delay. If verification or more time is needed, we explain why and provide an expected timeline.

The developer cannot remotely access or delete data held only on your device. Manage Google account and purchase information through the relevant service’s privacy settings and support channels.

10. Safeguards

Photos and game data use Android private app storage; automatic app backup is disabled. Sharing allows the selected destination to access the shared file. Support information is used for assistance and rights requests and deleted by the operator after the retention period. You can manage device locks and files shared elsewhere.

11. Website, email and international processing

The website has no site-operated analytics, ad scripts, tracking cookies, external web fonts, registration or photo upload. GitHub Pages processes visitor IP addresses and technical requests for hosting and security. The support composer opens an email draft; it does not submit or store the message on this website. Emails you send are processed by the operator and email providers such as Gmail.

Google provides advertising, Google Play and Gmail; GitHub provides website hosting. Information may be processed in other countries, including the United States. Locations and retention and deletion terms follow the Google Privacy Policy, Google retention policy and GitHub Privacy Statement. The developer does not specify each user’s external storage location or a uniform retention period.
